Frequently Asked Questions
What broker do I need?
Any MT5 broker works. We recommend brokers with tight spreads on gold (XAUUSD). Our EAs are currently running on VantageMarkets demo and live accounts. Make sure your broker supports the symbols you want to trade.
How much do I need to start?
Minimum recommended: $1,000 for a standard account, or $100 for a cent account. The EAs risk only 0.5% per trade, so even small accounts are protected. Prop firm accounts ($10K+) work great too.
Do I need to run a VPS?
Yes, for 24/5 operation. We provide a VPS setup guide. A basic Windows VPS costs $10-20/month. Alternatively, you can run MT5 on your local PC during trading hours.
What's the expected return?
We don't promise specific returns — anyone who does is lying. What we offer is a disciplined, risk-managed system with proven strategies. Our EAs target 2-5% monthly with strict risk controls. Past performance doesn't guarantee future results.
Can I use this with a prop firm?
Yes! Our EAs are designed with prop firm rules in mind: conservative risk per trade (0.5%), daily drawdown limits, and session-gated trading. ScalpXAU and FXPair are particularly popular with prop firm traders.
What if it doesn't work for me?
We offer a 7-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with the setup support or EA performance, contact us for a full refund. We want satisfied traders, not trapped customers.
How are updates handled?
We regularly optimize EAs based on market conditions. Updates are pushed automatically and you'll receive new .ex5 files via email or Telegram. All updates are free for active subscribers.
