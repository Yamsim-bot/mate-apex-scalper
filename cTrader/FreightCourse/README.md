# Freight Trading Course 📦

Learn the **freight (shipping rates) market** from zero — concepts first, then the market, then the derivatives.

> **What is freight?** It's the price of moving goods by sea. It's a real, tradable market — the **Baltic Dry Index (BDI)** is one of the most famous economic indicators in the world, and freight rates trade as **Forward Freight Agreements (FFAs)**.

## Why learn this

- It uses the same mental toolbox as your trading workspace: supply & demand, seasonality, hedging, and journaling.
- The BDI is a barometer of global trade — it reacts to what's happening in China and world shipping often before other indicators do.
- The structure (concepts → market → instruments → practice) is the same path you'd take for any new market.

## The learning path

**~2–3 weeks at 15–20 min/day.** One lesson per day, in order. Don't binge — each lesson ends with key takeaways; read them twice.

| Module | Lessons | What you'll be able to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundations | 01–03 | Explain what freight is, who trades it, and which ships carry which cargo |
| 2. The Market | 04–05 | Read the Baltic indices (BDI, BCI, BPI…) and explain what moves them |
| 3. FFA | 06–07 | Price and settle a Forward Freight Agreement; work a hedging example |
| 4. Practice | Glossary + Exercises | Test yourself and speak the language |

## How to use it (the easy-learning method)

1. **One lesson a day.** Each lesson is short on purpose — read it, then read the key takeaways once more.
2. **Do the exercises** in `04-Practice/exercises.md` after each module. Answers are hidden on purpose: attempt first, then check.
3. **Keep a journal.** Just like `Journals/Spotware/Journal-2026-08.txt` is your forex log, start `FreightCourse/journal.md` with one line per day: BDI level, biggest mover, why.
4. **Use the glossary** whenever you hit an unknown word — it's the vocabulary of the whole course.

## Connecting it to the rest of this workspace

- **Templates**: the `Range / Trend / Volatility` thinking from `Templates/` applies here too — is the BDI trending or ranging? That decides whether a freight idea is trend-following or mean-reversion.
- **Journaling**: same discipline as your trade journal — write the reason *before* the trade, the outcome *after*.
- **Trading it**: FFAs settle in cash against the Baltic index — no ships involved. This course is about understanding the market first; treat any live trading as a later, separate step.
