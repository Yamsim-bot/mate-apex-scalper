# Lesson 03 — Ships and cargoes

**Goal:** recognize the vessel classes, know what each carries, and see why size is the whole game.

## Dry bulk — the four main classes

| Class | Size (DWT) | Typical cargo | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Capesize** | ~150k–200k t | Iron ore, coal | Too big for the Panama Canal; long hauls (Australia/Brazil → China) |
| **Panamax** | ~60k–85k t | Coal, grains, minor bulks | The "standard" workhorse; fits the Panama Canal |
| **Supramax / Ultramax** | ~50k–64k t | Grains, fertilizers, steel products | Flexible; many ports |
| **Handysize** | ~25k–45k t | Small lots, niche cargoes | Fits smaller ports |

**DWT** = deadweight tonnes — how much cargo the ship can carry. Size matters because **cost per tonne falls as ships get bigger**: it's cheaper per tonne to move iron ore on a Capesize than on a Handysize.

## The world's most important cargo: iron ore

Roughly **70% of seaborne iron ore goes to China** (steelmaking). It mostly travels on Capesizes from:

- **Australia** (Port Hedland) → China, ~2-week haul — route **C5**
- **Brazil** (Tubarão) → China, ~6-week haul — route **C3**

This single trade is why the Capesize market (and the BDI) is so sensitive to Chinese steel and property demand.

## Other key flows

- **Coal** — thermal (power) and coking (steel): Capesize + Panamax.
- **Grains** — wheat, corn, soybeans: Panamax + Supramax; strong harvest seasonality.
- **Minor bulks** — bauxite, phosphate, cement, sugar, steel coils: Supramax + Handysize.

## Tankers (briefly)

| Class | Size | Cargo |
|---|---|---|
| **VLCC** | ~2M barrels | Crude — long hauls (Middle East → Asia) |
| **Suezmax** | ~1M barrels | Crude |
| **Aframax** | ~700k barrels | Crude & products, shorter hauls |

Same logic as dry bulk: bigger ship = cheaper per barrel, but fewer ports can take it.

## Key takeaways

- Four dry-bulk classes, big to small: **Capesize → Panamax → Supramax → Handysize**.
- Iron ore to China (routes C5, C3) is the center of gravity of the whole market.
- Bigger ships are cheaper per tonne but less flexible.
- Match cargo to ship: iron ore = Capesize, grains = Panamax/Supramax, oil = VLCC.
